SafeNest

A community-driven platform that helps reunite lost pets with their owners and gives found and stray pets a safer path to care and a new home. It brings lost-pet reporting, found-pet reporting, potential matching, community participation and rehoming into one connected experience. Interactive dashboards show each user only what is right for their role: lost reporter, found reporter or rehomer. It tackles shelter overcrowding, pet abandonment and the lack of a community platform for lost and rehomed pets.

CropShield

An offline-first weed helper for small farmers. Point a phone at a weed using a photo, video or live camera and get its name, a safe next step (gentler methods first) and a spoken answer in English, Telugu, Hindi or Bengali, with no internet and no server. It also has a field map to treat only weedy squares, saved weather and farm data, and a job board for finding weeding help. As creator, co-founder and team lead, I led a team of three, trained the MobileNet image model in Google Colab, built the React + Vite app with offline support and packaged the Android app. In our test of 5 weeds, accuracy was 98%.
